# Amal Jyothi College of Engineering Autonomous (AJCE)
## Official Academic Web Portal & Digital Experience

This repository hosts the official high-performance web application for **Amal Jyothi College of Engineering Autonomous (AJCE)**, Kanjirappally, Kerala, India.

---

### 🏛️ Institution Highlights
- **Conferred Autonomous Status**: By UGC on 6 July 2023
- **Affiliation**: APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU)
- **Accreditations**: NAAC 'A+' Grade | 7 NBA Accredited Programmes
- **Rankings**: KIRF (Kerala Institutional Ranking Framework) Rank #4 in Kerala (2026)
- **Campus**: 68-acre eco-smart campus, 1.26 lakh sq.m. built-up area
- **Innovation Ecosystem**: 2 DST-funded TBIs (Startups Valley & TBI-KEC) with 95+ startups incubated
- **AICTE IDEA Lab**: 1 of 49 sanctioned nationally across India
